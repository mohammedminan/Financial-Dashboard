> 🚀 **Financial Analytics Dashboard** :

Analyze company financial data to identify trends in sales, profit, COGS, regional performance, product-wise contribution, and growth over time.

---

📝 **Description/Purpose** :

This project aims to analyze **financial data** to uncover insights on sales, profit margins, discounts, regional performance, and year-over-year growth using interactive Excel dashboards.

---

🧰 **Tech Stack** :

• Microsoft Excel – Data cleaning, transformation, and visualization  

• Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts – For summarizing and analyzing large datasets  

• Slicers – To create interactive filters for country and year  

• Data Validation & Formatting – To enhance data usability and presentation  

---

📌 **Feature and Highlights**

• **Goal** :  
To extract meaningful business insights from company financial data by identifying trends in product-wise profit, sales categories, regional contribution, and time-based performance, and to present these insights through an interactive and visually engaging Excel dashboard.

• **Walkthrough on Key Visuals** :

**KPI Cards**: Display Total Sales, Total Profit, and Total COGS.  

**Donut Chart**: Shows sum of profit by region (West, East, South, Central).  

**Table (Conditional Formatting)**: Product-wise breakdown of profit and discounts with color-coded highlights.  

**Column + Line Chart**: Compares this year’s sales vs last year’s sales with growth % by year.  

**Waterfall Chart**: Highlights increase/decrease in sales by category and year.  

**Clustered Column Chart**: Sales, COGS, and Profit analysis by quarter.
